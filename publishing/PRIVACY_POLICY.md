# Privacy Policy — Fusion to Simscape Multibody

Effective date: 24 November 2026

Publisher: Omar Salem  
Contact: ars.automata.fusion@gmail.com

## Scope

This policy describes how the Fusion to Simscape Multibody add-in, version 0.1.0, and its included MATLAB helpers handle information. It also describes information you choose to send to the publisher for support.

## Local processing and exported files

The add-in reads the active Fusion design to export visible solid geometry, assembly structure and placement, document and component names, body identifiers, colours, mass properties, and supported joints and rigid groups. It writes XML, JSON, STL geometry, and MATLAB helper files to the export folder you select. These files can contain confidential design information.

The MATLAB helpers read these exported files and create local Simscape Multibody models, import data, manifests, and, when configured, simulation outputs. Previous generated models and data may be moved into a local history folder before replacement.

The add-in and included helpers do not send your designs or exports to the publisher. They do not include analytics, advertising trackers, telemetry, or a publisher-operated account system. The add-in does not require you to provide personal information to perform an export.

If you select a folder synchronised by a cloud-storage service, that service may upload the files according to your own settings. You control where exports are stored and with whom they are shared.

## Support correspondence

If you email the publisher, the publisher receives your email address and any message, diagnostic information, screenshots, or files you choose to include. This information is used to respond to your request, investigate problems, and maintain the add-in. Do not send confidential designs or personal information unless needed for your request and you are authorised to share them.

Support messages are handled through the publisher's email provider, Gmail, and may be processed or stored in countries outside your own. The publisher does not sell support information or use it for advertising. Information may be disclosed where required by applicable law.

## Storage and deletion

The publisher does not retain a copy of designs or exports merely because you use the add-in. You manage local exports, generated models, history folders, backups, and any synchronised copies. Uninstalling the add-in does not automatically delete these files.

Support correspondence may be retained while needed to resolve your request and maintain a relevant support record. Contact the publisher to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information in support correspondence. Requests will be handled subject to applicable legal obligations and technical limitations, including email-provider backups.

## Other products and services

Autodesk Fusion, the Autodesk App Store, MATLAB, Simscape, Simscape Multibody, your email service, and any cloud-storage service are separate products. Their providers' privacy policies govern information those products collect or process. This policy covers the add-in and publisher support described above, rather than those providers' independent practices.

## Changes and contact

This policy may be updated if the add-in's information-handling practices change. The effective date identifies the policy version. For privacy questions or requests, contact Omar Salem at ars.automata.fusion@gmail.com.
